  Throughout the development of this project, the importance of how intrinsic basic data cleaning steps are in allowing for trustworthy analysis. Standardizing text through stripping excess whitespace and fixing capitalization errors prevents repetition of categories, while filtering for non-positive values or values equal to zero removed what may have led to a distortion in revenue or inventory history later on. Handling missing values using forward or backward fill consistently as well as dropping unstable rows demonstrated how much these choices can alter the final data set and must be executed with deliberate caution and attention over pure automation.
